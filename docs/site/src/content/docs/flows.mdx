What a person does in Justus, one journey at a time.

- [First run](../first-run/): a new person opens Justus, gives a name and chooses to create or join an album.
- [Make an album](../make-an-album/): someone with photos to share creates an album and opens it.
- [Invite someone](../invite-someone/): the Admin lets a new person in with an invite link.
- [Join an album](../join-an-album/): someone with an invite link asks to join, and the Admin answers.
- [Share photos](../share-photos/): a member adds photos and decides who sees them.
