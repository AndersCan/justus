import Machine from "@mantaq/inspect/astro/Machine.astro";

Two people, two phones. Maya is a Member; Nils is the Admin. The page has no drives, so every save works there.

| Moment                                         | Maya sees                                                                                                | Nils (Admin) sees                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Maya archives the album                        | It leaves Your albums and shows under Archived `albumLifecycle.archived`                                 | Nothing changes                                                           |
| Maya restores it                               | It is back in Your albums `albumLifecycle.shownInYourAlbums`                                             | Nothing changes                                                           |
| Maya confirms Leave                            | The album and her photos go from her phone `albumLifecycle.leavingAlbum`                                 | Nothing yet                                                               |
| Maya's phone waits                             | Nothing of the album shows `albumLifecycle.waitingForAdminToSeeYouLeft`                                  | His phone hears it and drops her from the people list                     |
| Nils's phone has dropped her                   | The last kept drives are removed `albumLifecycle.removingYourLastCopy`                                   | The people list no longer lists Maya                                      |
| Nils confirms Delete album                     | Nothing yet                                                                                              | The album's photos go from his phone `albumLifecycle.deletingForEveryone` |
| Nils's delete is written                       | Nothing yet                                                                                              | Only the delete note is kept `albumLifecycle.deletedForEveryone`          |
| Maya's phone hears the delete, even days later | The copy is removed `albumLifecycle.removingDeletedAlbum`, then a notice `albumLifecycle.deletedByAdmin` | Nothing more                                                              |

**When it goes wrong.**

| What went wrong                 | State                                                    |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| The leave could not be written  | `albumLifecycle.shownInYourAlbums`, with the reason kept |
| The delete could not be written | `albumLifecycle.shownInYourAlbums`, with the reason kept |
