title: "Add a friend to an album"
description: "The Admin adds a saved friend without a link, and the friend joins from the albums list."

import Machine from "@mantaq/inspect/astro/Machine.astro";

Two people, two phones. Nils is the Admin; Kari is a saved friend. Both phones must have Justus open.

| Moment                                  | Nils (Admin) sees                                                                                               | Kari sees                                                                                              |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Nils taps "Add a friend"                | His saved friends who are not in the album, each with a tick box `addFriend.choosingFriends`                    | Nothing                                                                                                |
| Nils ticks Kari and Ole                 | "Add Kari and Ole as Viewers" or "Add Kari and Ole as Collaborators" `addFriend.choosingFriends`                | Nothing                                                                                                |
| Nils taps "Add Kari and Ole as Viewers" | "Asking Kari and Ole…" and "If someone doesn't have Justus open, their invite waits." `addFriend.askingFriends` | Nothing yet                                                                                            |
| Both phones take the invite             | "Asked Kari and Ole to join as Viewers"; the sheet closes `addFriend.showingPeopleWithAddFriend`                | The album at the top of her albums list, "Nils invited you" and Join `friendOffer.showingFriendOffers` |
| Kari taps Join                          | Nothing to do: no Accept step                                                                                   | "Joining “&lt;album&gt;”…" `friendOffer.joiningFriendsAlbum`                                           |
| Nils's phone answers                    | Kari under Members, as a Viewer                                                                                 | The album opens, and says she is a Viewer `friendOffer.showingFriendOffers`                            |

**When it goes wrong.**

| What went wrong                                | What the person sees                                                                                      | State                                        |
| ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Kari's phone does not know Nils                | Nils: "Couldn't add Kari from here. Send Kari a link instead."                                            | `addFriend.showingWhoWasAsked` (refused)     |
| Kari's phone is off or does not answer         | Nils: "Couldn't reach Kari. Ask Kari to open Justus, then try again."                                     | `addFriend.showingWhoWasAsked` (unreachable) |
| Nils's phone does not answer within 30 seconds | Kari: the row returns to Join, with "Couldn't join “&lt;album&gt;”. &lt;Admin&gt; needs Justus open too." | `friendOffer.showingFriendOffers`            |
| Nils took the invite back                      | Kari: the invite row goes away, with no message                                                           | `friendOffer.showingFriendOffers`            |
