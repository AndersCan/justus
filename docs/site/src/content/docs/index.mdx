## How it stays private

- **You accept every person.** An invite link only lets someone ask to join. They see no photos until you accept them.
- **Photos live on the phones in the album.** Not on a server, ours or anyone's: phones connect directly, peer to peer, over encrypted connections.
- **No ads, no tracking, no analytics.** No account or email either, just a name.
