Justus is a photo sharing app with no server in the middle.

Most photo apps upload your pictures to a company's servers, and everyone else downloads them from there. Justus works peer to peer: your photos go straight from your phone to the phones of the people in your album, and only to them. Nobody else can see them, including us.

ALBUMS FOR THE PEOPLE YOU CHOOSE
• Start an album and invite friends or family.
• You approve everyone who asks to join, and you can remove members.
• Everyone in the album sees all of its photos, including older ones.
• Add photos from your gallery or take them with the camera.

PRIVACY YOU CAN SEE
• No account, no sign-up, no cloud storage.
• Photos are stored on the phones in the album, nowhere else.
• Connections between phones are encrypted.
• The People tab shows who is connected right now and when everyone else was last seen.
• No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Photos travel while both phones have Justus open and can reach each other. If a friend's phone is off, their new photos show up the next time you are both online.
• Two phones on mobile data sometimes can't find each other. Switching one of them to WiFi usually helps.
• Your photos live on your devices, so a photo is safe as long as at least one phone in the album has synced it.

Justus is for sharing with friends and family. It holds only the photos you deliberately add, not your whole library.
